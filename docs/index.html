RejectBench

Reviewed by a machine set on rejecting your paper?

RejectBench is a research survey on malicious AI-generated peer reviews: reviews produced with language models to push a paper toward rejection rather than to assess it. We want to learn how researchers recognise them, how they experience them, and what should be done about them.
